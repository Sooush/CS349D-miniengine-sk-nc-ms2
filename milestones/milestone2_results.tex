\section{Page-size comparison}

\noindent Benchmark (same as previous section):
\begin{verbatim}
python3 -m benchmark.bench_serving --input-len 512 --output-len 256 \
  --randomness 1.0 --concurrencies 8,16,32 --num-requests 64
\end{verbatim}

\noindent Engines compared:
\begin{verbatim}
python -m miniengine --model Qwen/Qwen3-8B --mode paged \
  --mem-fraction-static 0.85 --page-size 256

python -m miniengine --model Qwen/Qwen3-8B --mode paged \
  --mem-fraction-static 0.85 --page-size 1024
\end{verbatim}

\begin{table}[htbp]
  \centering
  \caption{\texttt{bench\_serving} for two KV page sizes.}
  \resizebox{\textwidth}{!}{%
  \begin{tabular}{@{}rcccccccc@{}}
    \toprule
    Page size & Conc & TTFT$_{p50}$ & TTFT$_{p99}$ & Compl$_{p50}$ & Compl$_{p99}$ & TPOT$_{p50}$ & TPOT$_{p99}$ & GenTok/s \\
    \midrule
    \multirow{3}{*}{256}
      &  8 & 346 & 3620 & 21762 & 23187 & 83.6 & 105.8 & 90 \\
      & 16 & 2910 & 16273 & 24207 & 38861 & 89.8 & 100.4 & 136 \\
      & 32 & 15699 & 35190 & 42104 & 77424 & 123.1 & 442.1 & 134 \\
    \midrule
    \multirow{3}{*}{1024}
      &  8 & 286 & 3179 & 15806 & 21383 & 82.9 & 86.5 & 90 \\
      & 16 & 2711 & 11665 & 19794 & 32116 & 90.2 & 98.6 & 131 \\
      & 32 & 14171 & 28943 & 33296 & 59648 & 119.9 & 304.6 & 130 \\
    \bottomrule
  \end{tabular}%
  }
\end{table}

\paragraph{Comment.}
Smaller pages (256) reduce wasted KV capacity at sequence tails but grow page-table metadata and indexing work; larger pages (1024) do the opposite.
In this sweep, aggregate generation throughput (\textbf{GenTok/s}) is nearly identical at concurrency~8 (90 vs.\ 90); at concurrency~16 and~32, \textbf{page-size 256} yields higher throughput (136 vs.\ 131; 134 vs.\ 130), while \textbf{1024} shows lower median completion time at several points (e.g.\ Compl$_{p50}$ at conc.\ 8 and~32), reflecting a tradeoff between tail latency and sustained tokens/sec rather than a single ``always better'' setting.
